01 · WHAT IT IS

One question, one tier.

QOWNER combines a single question, a deliberately distracting clue and a tier system that sets the weight of the challenge. Built for anyone who enjoys a real test.

01

One question, not a long list

Each round rests on a single question, so the challenge is accuracy rather than reading speed.

02

A deliberately distracting clue

The clue exists to test you, not to make it easier. That is part of the design.

03

Tiers that set the weight

A tier sets how heavy the challenge in front of you is.

03 · LEADERBOARD

Empty, and stated plainly.

This page does not claim any players exist. The leaderboard on the quiz page is still empty: not a single entry. No numbers are invented to make it look busy.

  • Public names or pseudonyms are welcome on the leaderboard.
  • The QOWNER alias and leaderboard participation are set on the dashboard, on the same account profile.
  • The QOWNER alias is not a separate account, and it cannot change the GitHub or Google account you sign in with.
