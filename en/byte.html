PRODUCT · COMPANION

Byte assists, it does not replace.

Byte is the companion inside Void Scriptor. It is there to ask questions and to carry work forward. Its local connection is optional and runs only on your computer.

ProductsByteDownloadsDocsStatus
01 · WHAT IT DOES

A companion inside the workflow.

Byte appears as an interactive widget. It does one job: you ask and carry the work forward together, without switching applications.

01

Ask while you work

The Byte panel opens from the widget, so a question can be asked without leaving the page you are on.

02

Carry work forward

Byte is used to continue work in progress, not to replace your decisions.

02 · HOW TO OPEN IT

Two places to call Byte.

01

From the homepage

Open the Byte panel on the homepage. This widget can be opened without signing in.

02

From the dashboard

Once signed in, the Byte panel is also available on the dashboard alongside your account settings.

03 · LOCAL CONNECTION

The Byte connection on your computer.

This is the part that gets misunderstood most, so it is spelled out: the local connection is optional, it runs only on your computer at 127.0.0.1:8766, and it accepts requests only from allowed addresses.

  • The homepage can still use a remote connection or offline mode.
  • This is not a public service and is not exposed to the internet by this page.
  • Never paste a token, cookie or any secret into a bug report.

Storage details are in the Privacy Policy and in the getting started guide.

04 · HONEST LIMITS

What this page does not claim.

01

No public API

Byte is not a service third parties can call from outside this site.

02

No quality promise

Byte output depends on your configuration and the material you provide. This page promises no capability that has not been tested.

03

No version number

Byte runs as part of the website, so there is no Byte installer to download.
