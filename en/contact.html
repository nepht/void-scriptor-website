Frequently asked questions

Are the products free?

Void Scriptor Studio is currently available as a preview. Some other products are still coming soon or in beta; see the status labels on the homepage.

How do I report a bug?

Email voidscriptor@gmail.com with reproduction steps and your product version.

How do I request data or deletion?

Download your data or delete your account from the Data & privacy panel in the dashboard. The Delete account and server data requires explicit confirmation and deletes account data stored on the server; backup files already in Google Drive are not deleted. If you cannot access the dashboard, email us with the subject Permintaan data pribadi. We will verify account ownership before processing a manual request; technical details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I report copyright infringement?

Use the subject Laporan pelanggaran hak cipta and include the work identification, material location, why you are entitled to report it, your contact details, and a good-faith statement.

Are the accounts on this site authentic?

Yes. Sign-in now uses production authentication through GitHub or Google : we never receive your password. Account identity is stored for the website. Apps do not use the website session yet. Connections between apps and QOWNER will be verified before activation. The Sign out button revokes the server session and removes session credentials from the device in use. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Are versions besides Windows available?

Void Scriptor Studio is available for Windows. QOWNER Quiz Maker is being prepared as a mobile app for iOS and Android and is not available for download yet; Linux and macOS versions of Studio are also being prepared.

Note

We do not request passwords, card numbers, or verification codes by email. Ignore any message claiming to be from us that asks for them. See Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
